Student hackathons and campus research grants suffer from opaque reviews & delayed payouts. 
We fixed this by deploying an ultra-fast, zero-loss Milestone Escrow Engine on @solana. ⚡

🧵 2/3: 🛠️ How it works:
1️⃣ Sponsors lock grants into deterministic Solana PDAs
2️⃣ Student grantees submit cryptographic milestone proofs
3️⃣ University oracles/validators sign releases in <400ms (<$0.0005 fee)
4️⃣ Real-time notifications stream to Discord, Slack & Resend email via our event relayer!
